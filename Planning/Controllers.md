#### Overview
- Two controllers: one for ROV, one for arm
- Communicate over wifi to ground control computer

#### In Controller
- 2 Joysticks (x and y axis)
- EPO no power
- On/off switch
- Scroll wheel (?) for up/down
- Arming/disarming button
- 3 buttons for 3 speeds

#### Controller Components
- 2 joysticks
- EPO button
- switch
- scroll wheel
- 4 buttons
