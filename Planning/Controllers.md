#### Overview
- Two controllers: one for ROV, one for arm
- Communicate over wifi to ground control computer

#### In Controller
- 2 Joysticks (forward/backward, up/down, left/right, 3 joints in arm)
- EPO no power
- On/off switch
- Button/switch (arm open/closed, light on/off)
- Extra buttons/switches
