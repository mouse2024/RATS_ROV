# RATS_ROV  - Siren's Signal
Robotics Association of Tulsa Students, Underwater ROV Project

This year, we are competing in the 2027 National Underwater Robotics Competition (NURC). The theme is "Siren's Signal."

## Competition Tasks
1. Recover three bottles to discover the locations of hidden artifacts inside the cave.
1. Clean up the kelp forest and reunite a lost seahorse with its shoal.
1. Open the treasure chest and recover the crown.
1. Restore power by routing an adapter through the trench and connecting it to the power terminal.
1. Decode the siren signal activated when the cave's side entrance opens.
1. Use the decoded siren message to identify and deliver the correct coin to the offering shell.
1. Locate and document three hidden snapper figures using the ROV's onboard camera.
1. Identify the starfish present in the designated area.

## Repo Organisation
