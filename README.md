# RATS_ROV  - Siren's Signal
Robotics Association of Tulsa Students, Underwater ROV Project

This year, we are competing in the 2027 National Underwater Robotics Competition (NURC). The theme is "Siren's Signal."

## 🐡 Competition Tasks
1. Recover three bottles to discover the locations of hidden items.
    - Retrieve 3 bottles and bring to surface
    - Read messages to find X and Y cooredinates of 3 artifacts
    - Use coordinates to retrieve artifacts from the cave
    - Place artifacts on display shelf near mission entrance
1. Clean up a ‘kelp forest’ and retrieve a seahorse from a cave.
    - Remove trash from coral
    - Transfport trash to designated garbage collection zone
    - Retrieve seahorse from cave and transpoft to help forest with the shoal
1. Open a treasure chest and recover the crown inside.
    - Locate trident blocking treasure chest inside cave
    - Rotate trident 90 degrees to open chest
    - Retrieve crown and bring to surface
1. Restore power by routing an adapter and connecting it to the power terminal.
    - Using provided prop, navigate trench and route cable through 4 waypoints.
    - Confirm that each waypoint illuminates
    - Insert power adapter into power terminal on back wall of trench
1. Decode a morse code signal.
    - Connect hydrophone to audio system to listen to signal
    - Docde the message
    - Record decoded message
1. Use the decoded signal to identify and deliver the correct coin.
    - Use decoded message to identify correct coin
    - Retrive identified coin from the surface
    - Place coin inside the offering shell
1. Locate and document three hidden snapper figures using the ROV's onboard camera.
    - Search the pool for 3 snapper figures
    - Record sightings through onboard camera's live video feed
1. Identify the starfish present in a designated area based on color.
    - Observe designated area to idenfity starfish based on colors
